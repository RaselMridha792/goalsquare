## One camp, one curriculum

Every camp follows the same structure: five days, five elements. Day one belongs to basic technique, then one element is added each day without dropping the previous one. On day five all the content comes together in game forms.

## Who it is for

The camps are aimed at goalkeepers from **U11 to U19**. Groups are formed by level, not by year of birth – we watch the group on the first morning and split it afterwards.

## For coaches

A **coach workshop** runs in parallel at every location. Two days in which we do not explain the method but apply it: you plan a session, deliver it with a real group and receive feedback afterwards.

## Registration

Places are limited to 24 goalkeepers and 12 coaches per camp. Registration runs through the contact form – send us the location, year group and level, and we will come back with the details.
