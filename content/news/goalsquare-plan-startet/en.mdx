## Planning in the browser – no installation

For more than twenty years goalkeeper coaches have planned their sessions with our desktop software. **Goalsquare PLAN** is the next step: the complete session planner runs directly in the web browser. No setup, no USB key, no installation – you sign in and start.

## The new app is part of it

The new Goalsquare app for iOS and Android launches alongside it. Everything you plan in the browser is on your phone seconds later – including drill animations, coaching points and your own notes. You no longer need a sheet of paper on the pitch.

## AI-assisted session planning

PLAN knows the 5 core elements of the Goalsquare method and proposes matching drill sequences: you set the focus, the available time and the desired load, and PLAN builds a suggestion. You stay in full control at all times – every block can be moved, replaced or deleted.

## What about BASIC and PRO licences?

Nothing changes. The BASIC Edition and PRO Suite remain full products and continue to be maintained. PLAN is an additional option for everyone who prefers to work in the browser or plan a season together with several coaches.

## Pricing and launch

PLAN costs **€ 9.99 per month** or **€ 99 per year** including full app access. Launch is planned for early September. Use the "Plan online" button to join the launch list – we will let you know the moment it goes live.
