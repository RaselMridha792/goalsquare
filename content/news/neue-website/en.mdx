## Why a relaunch?

Our old site served us well for many years, but it had grown rather than been planned. Content sat in several places, the language versions drifted apart and on a phone a lot of it was uncomfortable.

## What is new

**An interactive concept section.** The 5 core elements are now clickable: one click on an element shows its description, its training content, an example drill and a video.

**A real shop.** Software, drills packs, bundles and media are in one place, with a cart and checkout instead of an order form.

**Four languages, one state.** German, English, French and Dutch are maintained together. No content exists in only one language any more.

**Built for the phone.** Most coaches open our site on the way to training. That is exactly what the site is now made for.

## What stays the same

The forum, the content of the method and all existing licences remain unchanged. If something is missing or you find a bug, write to us – we welcome every piece of feedback.
