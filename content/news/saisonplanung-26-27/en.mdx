## A season is not a stack of single sessions

The most frequent question we receive is: "How do I distribute the content across a year?" Our answer has always been the same – not by weeks, but by **elements**.

## The cycle in four phases

**Pre-season (6 weeks).** Focus on basic technique and footwork. This is where the foundation is laid: basic stance, movement to the ball, coordination. High load, low complexity.

**First half (16 weeks).** Ball protection and positioning move to the front. Basic technique stays as a short block in every session – never longer than ten minutes, but never skipped.

**Winter block (4 weeks).** Time for what the league schedule leaves no room for: sharpening falling technique, isolating weaknesses, video analysis.

**Second half (16 weeks).** Game play dominates. Everything learned comes together under pressure – keeper vs. keeper, keeper vs. team.

## A practical note

Plan a maximum of **two** focus elements per session. Three or more means no content is repeated long enough to stick. The software shows the distribution of your elements across the cycle in colour, so you can see at a glance whether an element is being neglected.
